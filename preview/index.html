Calibration & settings
Effective inertia — kg·m² solid cylinder: I = ½ m r²

Torque is estimated from the roller's angular acceleration: τ = I × α + τ_loss. Mass and radius set the inertia automatically; the moving-average window smooths omega before the derivative is taken. Coast-down calibration: click the button, spin the roller up with the motor unpowered, then let it coast. For 15 s the firmware averages −I × α while the roller slows and stores the result as friction torque. This is an inertial dyno estimate: it measures torque while speed changes, not steady-state torque. There is no load cell.
